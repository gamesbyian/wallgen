# Dense Typography Stress Proof

## Agent 2 T3n: eight-face, four-line maximum-density typography stress · 24 October 2026

**[PR #705](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/pull/705)** exercises the authored upper bound of the canonical Text system in one current-product composition. [Actual Linux source-Chromium GHA #40167429815](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/actions/runs/40167429815) **PASS** with **8 editable text objects**, **8 distinct licensed bundled fonts**, **4 × 147 codepoints per object** (**1,176 authored characters across 32 lines**). Modes included Draw, Hairpin, Loop, Wave, Arc, Circle, nested two-ring strokes, black/white directional shadows, gradient/word/letter fills and line spacing. All eight real fonts were fetched and successfully prepared. Actual 360×780 source artwork was directly viewed: extreme user-authorable density visibly piles curves, outlines and multiple fonts atop one another; this is deliberate stress artwork rather than a recommended layout or claim of attractive typography.

**Quantitative acceptance:** eight-font preparation measured **115.7ms**, synchronous direct 360×780 render **64.6ms**, fresh worker PNG **343.9ms** (552,876-byte image) on that specific GitHub Linux Chromium runner. Direct/worker **0 differing colour channels**, and **513,048** colour channels differed versus the same wallpaper without lettering. The actual app Download handler created a **3024×6552** PNG (**12,975,229 bytes**) in **1,497ms**, with all eight editable objects, texts, original font IDs, path types and authored shadow angles surviving metadata extraction exactly. No page exceptions, browser crashes or worker disconnects. Initial application/preview portion measured **1,177ms** on the runner. These are **one-run timings only**, not stable benchmarks; the full-size PNG file's compression size says nothing definitive about peak in-memory Canvas/worker allocation or physical Android success.

**Conclusion:** no demonstrated software correctness, worker parity or export-integrity blocker was found at maximum canonical density in the tested environment; therefore no speculative renderer rewrite or larger GHA test factory is justified by this run. Dense-lettering artistic overlap remains a legitimate creator-authored choice, and needs human judgement in final review. Retain opt-in manual `scripts/dense-typography-browser-proof.mjs` for debugging; the temporary heavy workflow was **removed after source-pixel inspection**.

**Explicitly untested:** Pixel 6 Pro physical Chrome/PWA memory, GPU/CPU throttling, thermal pressure, touch responsiveness/IME, TalkBack, user preference and actual creator acceptance. The 12.98MB PNG is not evidence those device constraints have been met. Issue #653 remains open for independent acceptance and contextual complex-script shaping.
