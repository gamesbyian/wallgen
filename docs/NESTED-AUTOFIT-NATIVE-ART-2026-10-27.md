The preview and full-native reference/worker pairs match **byte-for-byte** for all three, all recover their normalized embedded recipes, and every shape has real nonempty native 1:1 RGB-ink crops (39,704 / 57,053 / 20,304 pixel counts, respectively). Each requested hue actually paints at least 93 high-chroma preview pixels. Full-native red/blue contours and pointed Delta apex were **viewed** as generated images rather than inferred from score or filename; the Δ's apex shows smooth rounded joins without stray isolated dots in its actual 1:1 crop. The skinny ellipse retains clean, visibly parallel concentric lines and a substantial inner violet outline.

**Truthful visual limits:** The cloud's third contour level splits (levels component counts 1,1,2,1,1,1,1); the Delta's hole and taper generate multiple legitimate contour components (2,2,2,2,2,4,2). Narrow medial-axis topology must not be forcibly bridged and is not a bug by itself. The **cloud violet and Delta violet are quantitatively small** (about 6.4% and 2.9% of their red pixel count) even though the status correctly reports seven surviving levels. This is a concrete creator-facing optical/readability concern, **not** proof that hue is missing, a broken renderer, or an entitlement to seven continuous closed loops. Native source images demonstrate the same asymmetry; investigate a *bounded, optional and explicitly confirmed* Auto-fit readability criterion or notice only if alternative actual rendered art measurably improves it without sacrificing normal gap and line visibility.

**Not verified:** physical Pixel performance/thermal load, TalkBack, Android IME, owner/user artwork, human aesthetic approval, absolute perceptual superiority or uniqueness of source generation parameters. Native runner timings here are individual Linux process observations, not mobile latency benchmarks. The one-shot heavy workflow must be deleted before merge; keep the manual proof script and this finite visual receipt.
