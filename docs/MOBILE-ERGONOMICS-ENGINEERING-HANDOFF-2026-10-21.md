## Delivered, verified in automated source browsers

- **Navigation:** Seven category tabs now have a regular equal-cell rhythm on ≤499px portrait, with full native 44px target height, no text spillage or hidden second row, and real horizontal finger swipe at narrower widths. The actual user screenshot's rightward bunching led to [#424](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/pull/424), 18/18 exact-head CI green. Five mobile destinations, optional Focus and named tools remain present.
- **Tall-phone controls:** Camera, zoom/Fit, randomization and preview rails, Focus and named Tool chips, Draw/Undo, three-layer Material rotation and its separate scoped controls, plus the original **Path Shape frequency** and **Shapes Stripe width** setting rows were selectively enlarged to independent ~44px cells where measured and qualified. This includes the 500–790px tall-portrait handoff, not just a 412px mockup. Relevant source closure PRs include [#393](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/pull/393), [#395](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/pull/395), [#400](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/pull/400), [#404](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/pull/404), [#411](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/pull/411), [#416](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/pull/416), [#419](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/pull/419) and [#421](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/pull/421). The patterns are deliberately **typed**, not an unvalidated blanket change to every dense setting.
- **Elements and precision authoring:** The original Interior Elements chooser, add/remove, reorder, enabled and lock actions have independent qualified controls on tall phones through 790px ([#414](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/pull/414), [#418](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/pull/418)); Draw has 44px-tall original actions on those tall screens ([#406](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/pull/406), [#421](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/pull/421)). Touchscreen Drawn anchor/handle priority, nearby distinct authored anchors 9–16px apart, point insertion/deletion, local point Undo and full source Apply/Undo were tested and repaired where necessary ([#402](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/pull/402), [#403](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/pull/403), [#412](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/pull/412)).
- **Cross-feature integrity:** A mobile browser journey exercised real Random → Undo → Path edit and property lock → Favourite → Random with lock retained → Saved restore → reload → actual PNG download. The embedded normalized recipe was extracted from the downloaded PNG bytes and matched exactly ([#407](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/pull/407)).
- **Reviewability:** Source screenshots, computed target geometry, real nine-point hit ownership and edge taps, disabled/locked actions, accessibility scans, high-DPI, desktop crossover and canonical visual plates gate the individual patches. Passing Chromium/mobile emulation **does not** equal physical Android or a human judgement.

## Still open: ranked by practical significance

1. **Physical Pixel 6 Pro acceptance and visual decisions (human-only).** Test installed-PWA versus Chrome in browser, maximum usable full-screen area, persistent unobtrusive exit control, Android Back/system-gesture interaction, thumb accuracy at cell edges, scrolling near the phone edges, TalkBack speech and focus order, 150–200% text scaling, both cream/dark themes and the chosen Focus/All ergonomics. See [#365](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/issues/365). None of these was carried out on a real device by this agent.
2. **Concrete compact 500×700 Draw occlusion (automatable; specific bug).** An original action panel at x60–440,y339–633 hides an actual authored anchor and overlaps side rails. [#425](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/pull/425) measured a temporary right 180px sidecar that freed **one** anchor with no rail intersection, but other points on the right could become blocked. **Do not ship that candidate without testing varied point and handle positions, scroll/focus/Undo/Cancel, short and tall crossover and both themes.** Track [#422](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/issues/422).
3. **Remaining generic dice, locks, steppers, colours and complex setting rows (automatable, only if prioritized).** Some original controls, especially shorter viewports, are still ~21–35px. The two individually qualified Path and Shapes rows and separate Material layout are **not** evidence for every other conditional setting. Inventory/measure actual visible target rectangles, text scaling and neighbour ownership; group by source row type before any redesign. Source acceptance, exact full-recipe parity and disabled/lock truth are mandatory. Keep this on [#365](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/issues/365), rather than create dozens of open speculative PRs.
4. **Precision ambiguity beyond verified cases (automatable with a real UX choice).** Two authored anchors closer than the six-pixel centre selection core, dense three-way clusters and overlapping point/handle affordances are not physically proven disambiguated. Prefer a selected-point cycle, offset magnifier or explicitly testable interaction after real user error evidence; do not silently enlarge overlapping canvas hotspots.
5. **Low-priority extreme cases:** Very short phone portrait retains compact actions; extreme 600×360 phone landscape remains separately nonblocking in [#301](https://github.com/gamesbyian/wallgen/issues/301). Do not sacrifice the normal portrait editor or artwork for these unless the product priority changes.
