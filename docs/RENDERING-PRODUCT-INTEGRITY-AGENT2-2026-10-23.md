## Source-level defect and fix

The forward-render path was allocating an uncached transparent ink canvas, texture compositing scratch surface and per-material raster canvases, then leaving their Canvas2D backing stores nonzero after the draw finished, pending eventual garbage collection. Main-thread preview fallback similarly retained deleted DOM canvases, because its cleanup only called `ImageBitmap.close()`. At 280 × 607, source-instrumented, *real* Chromium rendering of the complex recipe below revealed **six stranded temporary canvases, 6,990,200 retained backing pixels (27,960,800 RGBA bytes)**. These are sized canvas pixel stores, **not** measured OS RSS or JavaScript heap.

PR #632 resets each per-material canvas after `drawImage`, releases uncached ink and scratch in an exception-safe `finally`, preserves the two surfaces explicitly owned by settled preview, and releases replaced fallback DOM preview canvases. A cheap Node fault-path contract joins `check:quick`; the opt-in `tests/render-surface-lifecycle-browser.mjs` records actual source-render behavior. No new material, thread, Photo preset, importer, recipe schema or reconstruction algorithm.
