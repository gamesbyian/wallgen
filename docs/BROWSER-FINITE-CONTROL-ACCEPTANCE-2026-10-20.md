# Finite original-control runtime acceptance (20 October 2026)

## Acceptance decision

The top-level source-control reachability queue item is accepted for **finite control identity and source-backed interaction**, rather than asserting exhaustive Cartesian combinations of all settings. This gate asks whether every historical original control or supported successor exists and genuinely works in at least one applicable state. The separate **inactive/locks**, **canonical screen conditionals**, **adversarial**, **accessibility** and **human visual** items continue to track contextual and subjective quality. This preserves the interpretation that source inventories alone never establish reachability.

## What this checkbox does *not* close

- **All simultaneous permutations:** 87 scalar settings and dynamic Interior entries form an enormous conditional space. Additional interaction chains and adversarial negative cases are open under the inactive/lock and screen-plate acceptance items. The source action census proves **one applicable interaction per source control**, not each combination.
- **Human-readable inactive explanations:** browser values, semantic names and deterministic lock state are proven for representative cases, but actual human comprehensibility is separately pending.
- **Physical inputs and assistive output:** no physical Android Chrome, TalkBack, OS picker/clipboard performance or actual spoken screen-reader acceptance occurred. Keyboard focus, touch-target geometry and axe/ARIA tests continue under the accessibility item.
- **Visual and semantic approval:** the eight unwired icon candidates and all complete screen/row plate visuals still await human/independent optical review. Do not interpret a passing source selector or screenshot atlas as design approval.

The finite source-operation reachability parent is therefore closed, **without** promoting any of those unrelated conditional, accessibility, visual or user-testing gates. All named interaction suites are merged to main with independent head-specific CI. The appendix itself has no runtime code and makes no new blanket product-release assertion.
