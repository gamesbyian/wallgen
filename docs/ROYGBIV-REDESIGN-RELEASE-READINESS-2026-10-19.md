## Open acceptance boundaries, not numerical checkboxes

1. **Visual agreement:** Compare actual implementation against every canonical plate and every important conditional state; explicitly approve or refine the eight unwired SVG candidates. A screenshot existing is not approval.
2. **Complex-tool full journeys:** Draw/Interpret/anchors/corners/resmoothing/extrapolation/weave/Cancel/Undo and all Material/Interior locking and persistence combinations. Target specific counterexamples instead of declaring completion from a passing subset.
3. **Export full matrix:** Native-preview parity across all applicable structures, textures, camera settings, combinations and interrupted/racing actions, with qualified foreground pixels.
4. **Accessibility and device:** True screen-reader labels and announcements, contrast, 320px minimum and Pixel 6 Pro Android Chrome hands-on checks; automated desktop Chromium emulation cannot replace physical device results.
5. **Release closure:** Review remaining unchecked queue items, annotate explicit exceptions, and obtain user's visual acceptance without rewriting historical source inventories or crediting conditional controls that do not exist.
